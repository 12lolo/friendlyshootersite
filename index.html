Update 0.945 · out now on itch.io

The Glow-Up Update

0.9.4 rebuilt how the game plays. This one is about how it looks and feels: nearly every menu was redone, everything got a bit of glass and glow, and the ending finally runs on the music it was written for. A few new faces moved in too, and some of them are hiding.

  • 5new characters

  • Almost every screen is new: the main menu, world select, character select, leaderboards, achievements and the loading screen.
  • Five new characters, one for every rarity, each with their own weapon, abilities and prestige perk.
  • Six secret characters are hidden in the game. You can't spin for them, you have to earn them.
  • Duel opponents learn how you fight and change their plan to beat you.
  • The tutorial is a real boot camp now, with clear steps and markers.
  • A new Aero style: glossy glass menus with a soft green glow, and an old-computer startup screen. Both can be turned off in Options.
  • The Gauntlet got a lot more fun: combos, room ranks, finishing-blow slow-mo, 12 new relics and the new God rarity.
  • Flags have a new look, a proper pickup and on-screen markers that show you where the rest are.
  • The ending has new voiced dialogue set to a new soundtrack piece, and the climax got a calmer new first act.

New characters

  • Lumberjack (Common): throws his axe like a boomerang and stuns whatever it hits. Right click chops. Abilities: Timber and Axe Storm.
  • Harpooner (Uncommon): a pump shotgun, plus a grappling hook on right click that pulls you to walls and ledges. Abilities: Anchor Drop and Kraken Lines.
  • Slugger (Rare): hold to charge a swing, land a HOME RUN, and swat enemy bullets and rockets out of the air. Right click pitches a bouncing baseball. Abilities: Batting Practice and Walk-Off.
  • Singularity (Epic): plasma bolts charge up a black hole that pins enemies in place and crushes them. Abilities: Phase Step and Event Horizon.
  • Titan (Legendary): a hammer that sends out a stunning shockwave, and a leap that slams down harder the higher you jump from. Abilities: War Cry and Meteor.
  • Each one has its own prestige perk: Timber, Deep Sea, Grand Slam, Event Horizon and Earthshaker.

Secret characters

  • Six characters are not in any spin. They show up as ??? in character select under the new SECRET filter, with a hint about how to get them.
  • Each one plays completely differently, with their own weapon, abilities and prestige perk.
  • If you already did what it takes, some of them unlock as soon as you open the menu.
  • One for free: beat every opponent in the Duel at least once.

Combos and ranks

  • Kills chain into a combo. From x3 every kill pays extra scrap, every 5th gets a callout and every 10th heals you.
  • Every fight room gets a rank from S to C, based on damage taken, speed and your best combo. An S rank gives a token.
  • The last kill in a room slows time down for a moment.
  • Health packs fly to you when you're hurt, from much further away once the fight is over.

New relics

  • Common: First Strike, Adrenaline and Steady Rhythm.
  • Rare: Vampiric, Frenzy, Big Game Hunter and Shrapnel.
  • Legendary: Berserker and Dragon's Hoard.
  • Power shrines are stronger, offer three abilities to pick from, and have three new ones: Meteor Shower, Soul Reaper and Aegis.

God relics

  • A new rarity above Legendary with two relics: Divine Judgement and Avatar of War.
  • Look for the rare God shrine from floor 2 on. Bosses can drop one from floor 3, and a Chance shrine might, if you're very lucky.

New HUD

  • A run plate at the top shows the floor, theme, objective and the rooms left.
  • Scrap, tokens, the map, your relics, the combo meter and room ranks all use the new HUD style.

Main menu

  • Cleaner layout: two tidy columns, a bigger START and the Challenges card on the right.
  • "Playing as" with your name, coins and characters unlocked at the bottom.
  • The whole menu works with the arrow keys or WASD.

World select

  • A list of every world, grouped into Training, Story, Modes and Extras, with LOCKED and CLEARED tags.
  • An info card with a description, what unlocks the world and your best for that mode: Gauntlet floor, Escape time, duel wins or Quick Levels beaten.
  • The 3D preview sits next to the list now. Click a world to pick it, double-click to play, or use W/S, A/D and the number keys.

Character select

  • Every character in one grid with rarity filters, showing what you own, what's prestiged and what's equipped.
  • A detail panel with a live 3D model, abilities with icons, the signature perk and legendary powers.
  • Clicking a character equips it straight away. Double-click or Enter to play.

Leaderboards

  • New look with a podium for the top 3 and a bar that shows your own rank and how far you are from the next player.
  • New boards: Gauntlet (deepest floor) and Escape (fastest time).
  • Boards load faster and can be refreshed with R.

Loading screen

  • Glass look with a spinning loader and a light running along the progress bar.

Achievements

  • Every achievement has a new badge: the colour shows its category and the rim shows its tier, from steel up to prismatic. Locked ones show a padlock version.
  • New achievements for 20 characters, prestiging 10 characters, 2,500 and 5,000 kills, 50 kills with each new character, and one hidden one for the secret characters.
  • "Collect all characters" now means all 30 spin characters, and "Unlock 10 Abilities" really needs 10.

Frutiger Aero

  • Menus, buttons and HUD panels are glossy glass now, still in FriendlyShooter green.
  • Glass pixels, light ribbons and soft glints drift behind the menus.
  • Turn it off under Options > Video > Style > Aero theme.

Startup screen

  • The game boots up like an old computer: a BIOS screen, then the FriendlyOS splash with its own startup sound.
  • Quitting shuts it down the same way.
  • Any key skips it, or turn it off under Options > Video > Style > Startup screen.

Kill callouts

  • DOUBLE KILL and the rest are a glass pill with an orb counter, glints and a bar that runs down while you can still chain the next kill.
  • The text that pops up over enemies has a cleaner outline and a soft glow.

Gallery

  • Glass panels, orb buttons and a glowing stage with floating glass cubes under the model. Same layout as before.
  • A now-playing bar with a visualizer, a seek bar and play/pause. The music plays through the game's own soundtrack player.

Duel select

  • The duel select screen is glass and green too.

Everything above goes back to the classic look when the Aero theme is off.

Somewhere in the terminal

  • Someone left something behind. Make yourself at home.

Tutorial

  • A 12-step boot camp: look, walk, sprint, jump, talk, shoot, dash and wall jump, all the way to the flag.
  • A card at the top right tells you what to do next. Arrows on the floor, beacons and signs with your own keys show you where.
  • Falling off doesn't kill you anymore. You go back to the last checkpoint.

Duels

  • Opponents learn: they remember how you move, which range you like, where you peek from and how you play each character.
  • Every fight they pick a plan (push, kite, flank, hold an angle...), dodge your shots, aim ahead of you and throw grenades.
  • The HUD shows how well they READ YOU, they call out what they've picked up, and the select screen shows what they already know about you.
  • Your opponent's health is a plate at the top in the new HUD style, with damage numbers and a timer and streak (or the score online).
  • Reset Progress > Modes makes them forget everything.

Final boss

  • Close-range characters get help in the final boss fight: the hands stay down longer after a slam, your hits on them count more and you take less damage.
  • Every character gets a minimum amount of health for that fight.

Flags

  • New look: a waving flag on a pole with a glowing gem, a ring on the ground and a beam of light you can see from far away.
  • Picking one up plays a chime that climbs with every flag, with sparks, a flash and a FLAG CAPTURED banner.
  • On-screen markers show how far away each flag is, with an arrow to the nearest. Turn them off under Options > Flag markers.
  • The last flag in a level slows time down for a moment.

Quick Levels

  • New loading and title pictures for all five Quick Levels.

The ending

  • New voiced dialogue, timed to the new soundtrack piece Out of this space.
  • The music dips under the voice so you can hear every line.

The climax

  • A new, calmer first act. No spoilers.
  • The camera moves more naturally, with breathing, head movement and footsteps.
  • Typing on the phone uses an on-screen keyboard now, and the ending is quieter.
  • Gauntlet: health packs, floating cubes and leftover effects stayed behind when you went to the next floor.
  • Enemies: some enemies left empty objects and death effects behind in every level after they died.
  • Flags: one flag could count twice.
  • Leaderboards: boards sometimes failed to load right after starting the game.
  • Airstrikes: airstrike and nuke planes flew straight through tall buildings and hills. They climb over them now.
  • Gallery: the first song you played froze the game for a few seconds, the song description could spill out of its card, and the Gallery music could stay silent.
  • Achievements: character and ability milestones showed outdated numbers.
  • Other: "Quick lev" is called "Quick Levels" in world select, plus lots of smaller fixes.
