Your Squad

You begin every run with Pistol and one random common ally. Each stage is 3 waves — the last is always a boss wave — and you spin the reward wheel after every wave. Fight through the Forest, Desert, City, an Elite Burst boss, a door-lined corridor, and the Gable & Goble final showdown. Choose Story Mode for the full campaign, or Infinite Mode for endless waves with a squad of up to 8.
